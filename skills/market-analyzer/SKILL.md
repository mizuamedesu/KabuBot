# market-analyzer

Use this skill when turning KabuBot signals into a readable market-open summary.

## Analysis Shape

- Start with a one-line headline.
- Rank the hottest tickers by anomaly score plus X social heat.
- Explain why the move may be an overreaction, a legitimate repricing, or simply insufficiently evidenced.
- Connect price features to social narrative without implying causality unless evidence supports it.
- End with what to monitor next: price levels, volume, filings, earnings, guidance, analyst notes, or further X narrative changes.

## Safety

- This is research support, not financial advice.
- Avoid direct buy/sell/hold instructions.
- Do not hallucinate SEC filings, earnings dates, posts, or catalysts.
- If Grok X search is disabled, explicitly state that social evidence is unavailable.
